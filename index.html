ABOUT ME

Hi, I’m Sergio, thanks for stopping by. I’m a photographer who’s been working with a camera for over 15 years, and I love capturing spaces, objects, and artwork in a way that feels clean and elegant. What I’m drawn to most are the lines, shapes, and shadows that give each subject its own character.

Over the years I’ve had the chance to work with some great people and brands, including Bvlgari, Casa Wabi, COS, Grupo Habita, IHG Hotels & Resorts, IKEA, Kimpton Hotels, Presidente Intercontinental and TAX Alberto Kalach, and my photos have appeared in publications like Dezeen, Wallpaper, Vogue, Monocle, Rizzoli, The Architectural Review and GQ.

My life in photography started out in the countryside, photographing animals and insects, and that curiosity for details, light, and how things fit together has never really left me. I’m a bit of a gear nerd too. I love experimenting with cameras, lighting, and the technical side of photography.

Outside of photography, I enjoy diving into books about mythology, psychology, and science fiction, or sketching to unwind.

If you’d like to collaborate or just say hi, you can reach me at hola@sergiolopezfoto.com. I’d be happy to hear from you.
